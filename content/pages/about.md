**This is a starter page. The personal introduction is still to come.**

This corner of the internet is a place for more than a list of titles. It can hold the things you make, the ideas you're working through, experiences that matter, and unexpected detours along the way.

## A few things to put here

Write a short introduction in your own voice. What are you curious about? What are you making or learning? What would you like someone visiting this page to understand about you?

Add your education, experience, or current interests when you are ready. You can link to entries with more detail, and keep the short version for your CV.

## The rest can grow

There is no requirement to have every chapter finished before writing the first one. Keep the work honest, the notes useful, and leave space for what comes next.
