title: "SSEmoNet-XAI: A Self-Supervised Multimodal Audio-Visual Framework for
  Interpretable Emotion Recognition"
section: research
summary: >-
  Abstract

  Efficient Human Computer Interaction (HCI) depends heavily upon effective
  integration of visual and audio emotion cues. Multimodal approaches have found
  themselves wanting in terms of integration in most cases and are usually
  limited by large datasets which are extensively manually labeled. In light of
  this, a novel two step self supervised learning approach has been proposed in
  order to circumvent this problem. In our approach, we train the model to learn
  relationships between audio and video cues separately. Data augmentation was
  used during training; some examples include masking out small parts of audio
  clips and shifting video frames to train the model to construct robust cross
  modal models. This is done to establish an effective baseline from which to
  start our experiments. These efforts have paid off, with the final model
  achieving an impressive 95.5% accuracy on eight different emotion categories.
  This technique can be employed effectively in real time emotion detection and
  identification in upcoming HCI systems.
