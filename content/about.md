I'm a Computer Science and Engineering graduate from **Ahsanullah University of Science and Technology (AUST)** with an interest in building practical software and exploring data-driven solutions.

I enjoy working on problems that can be turned into useful, well-designed applications. One example is **SpaceTabs**, a browser-based tab and workspace manager I developed to address my own workflow challenges.

My interests extend beyond software development to **data analysis, machine learning, artificial intelligence, and computer science research**. I enjoy exploring different technologies, understanding how systems work, and continuously expanding my technical knowledge.

I value practical problem-solving, thoughtful engineering, and building things that serve a genuine purpose.

This portfolio brings together some of my projects, research, and academic background.
