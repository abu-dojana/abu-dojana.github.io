**Example entry — no employment, volunteering, or qualification is being claimed.**

Experience can take different forms. Paid work, a research contribution, a club responsibility, volunteering, or practical training can each deserve a clear account.

## The context

Explain where the work happened and what needed to be done. Clearly state whether it was employment, volunteering, training, or an independent activity.

## Your contribution

Describe the part you personally handled. Include how you approached it, who you worked with when relevant, and evidence you can share.

## What changed

Write about a concrete result or a lesson you can explain. Keep planned training and qualifications accurately labeled; list a license as earned only after it has actually been awarded.
