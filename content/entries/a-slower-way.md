**Example entry — replace this with your own adventure.**

Some stories begin with a destination. Others begin with a decision to take the longer route.

This sample shows how a simple outing can become a field note: a few photographs, a small observation, and something to remember. An adventure can be a long journey or an unfamiliar street close to home.

## What caught your attention?

Write about a detail that would be easy to miss. The light on a building, a conversation, a route you had never tried, or a moment that changed the plan.

> A useful note doesn't have to explain everything. Sometimes it only needs to preserve what made you stop and look.

## What would you keep?

- One thing you noticed.
- One thing that surprised you.
- One thing you would try differently next time.
