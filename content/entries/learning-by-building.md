**Example article — use this space for your own writing.**

There is a particular kind of understanding that arrives only after the first attempt goes wrong.

This sample article demonstrates a longer note. You can use the same space for a blog post, a technical explanation, an opinion, a reading note, or something that doesn't fit a familiar category.

## A question worth keeping

When a lesson makes sense, ask: *could I use this without following the example?* That question can become a small project, and the project can become an explanation for someone else.

## Leave a trail

Keep the failed attempt as well as the final solution. Explain what confused you and what changed your understanding. That record can be more useful than a polished conclusion on its own.

Your writing can grow with you. Categories and menus can change without forcing the story into a different template.
