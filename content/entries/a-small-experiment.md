A good experiment starts small enough to finish and clear enough to explain. This sample entry is a place to document that process.

## Start with a question

What do you want to understand? Explain the question in a sentence, then describe why it is worth investigating.

## Make the first test simple

Describe your data or inputs, the baseline, and your method. Include code or a chart when it helps the reader understand the work.

```python
# A tiny example, not a project result.
```

## Be precise about the result

Explain what worked, what failed, and what you still cannot conclude. A limitation is useful information. Add actual measurements only when you have measured them.
